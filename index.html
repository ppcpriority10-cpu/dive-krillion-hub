Dive Krillion

Dive Krillion is a free daily word game you play in the browser. A new dive arrives every day, and everyone in the world gets the same 7 prompts. There is no signup and no download. You type one answer per prompt, and the rarer your answer, the deeper you dive.

Play today's dive free

How it works

Each daily dive has 7 prompts with 25 seconds each. Answers are scored by rarity, from Plankton at 10 meters for the most common answers up to One in a Krillion at 100 meters for answers almost nobody thinks of. A perfect dive reaches 7,000 meters. Your depth meter sinks through five ocean zones as you play, from Sunlight down to the Hadal zone.

What you get

  • A new daily dive every day at midnight New York time — the same prompts for everyone, so you can compare with friends.
  • Past dives you can replay any time you missed a day.
  • Unlimited play between dailies, plus daily answers and hints for every date.
  • A guest leaderboard — no account needed, your best dives are remembered on your device.
  • Shareable results — post your depth without spoiling the answers.

Why rare answers win

Most word games reward the first answer that comes to mind. Dive Krillion rewards the opposite: the answer nobody else thinks of. That turns every prompt into a small creative puzzle. "Name a fruit" is easy. Naming a fruit nobody else names is the game.
